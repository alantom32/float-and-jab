Seven moves.
One flow.

Every fighter starts here. Watch the stick man, then copy him slowly in the mirror.

01Relax

Tight shoulders slow your hands. Breathe out on every punch and keep your grip soft until impact.

02Rhythm

Bounce lightly on the balls of your feet. Fighters who move to a beat never get stuck flat-footed.

03Repeat

Ten clean jabs beat a hundred sloppy ones. Slow, correct reps become fast, automatic ones.
